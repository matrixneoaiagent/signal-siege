The rules in one breath: on your turn, claim an empty cell adjacent to your territory, fortify one of your cells, or attack an adjacent enemy cell. Attack power is 1 plus the number of your cells touching the target, and a capture succeeds only if that beats the target’s strength; a failed attack still spends your turn. Own any three of the four signal nodes to win instantly — if time expires first, the highest score wins, with nodes, total strength, and seat order as tiebreakers.
